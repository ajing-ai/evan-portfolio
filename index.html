I'm Evan K., a presentation designer working across corporate communication, product launches and large-format event visuals.

I turn dense information into clear visual narratives — from company overviews and technical product stories to English-language B2B presentations. Structure, typography and image direction work together to make the message easy to follow.

The portfolio brings together 15 projects across technology, materials, corporate reporting and global commerce. The original slides remain in their supplied language, with English project context throughout.
